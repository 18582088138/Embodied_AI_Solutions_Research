# 02 Intel 具身智能软件栈总览

截止 2026-11-05。本章沿系统驱动、异构推理、策略运行、感知导航、实时执行与数据训练六条路径展开，专题各自提供源码/API、官方图、本地原件与检查记录。Intel 的平台价值是把 x86 主机、CPU/iGPU/NPU、ROS 和工业控制接口组织成可集成的组件集合。公开材料仍有版本漂移、未闭环的模型转换语义和量产运维缺口，不能将组件可下载等同整机已验收。

本章为文献和源码调研，未安装 SDK、转换模型、训练策略或测试机器人。01 已审阅内容及 03—09 文件保持不变；本章完成后等待审阅，不开始 03。专题索引提供全部正文与证据入口。

## 从源模型到传感器和动作的两条链

视觉检测路线：模型/标签→转换 IR→显式设备编译→图像预处理→OpenVINO inference request→检测消息→costmap 或 grasp。launch 默认 GPU，并将 engine、输入/标签路径写入 YAML；CPU/GPU/NPU 参数说明候选目标，不证明 DMA 可达性和端到端零拷贝。PCL/FLANN 以 SYCL queue、同 context 的点云与驻留查询缓冲降低重复分配/搬运，仍需检查 ROS 序列化、图像/点云布局和每阶段同步。[Intel02D_Launch](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/robotics-ai-suite/1b0b22a2964f0f998c34cc03c5053fe61764015d/src/components/object-detection/src/object_detection_tutorial/launch/openvino_object_detection.launch.py) [Intel02D_PCL](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/robotics-ai-suite/1b0b22a2964f0f998c34cc03c5053fe61764015d/docs/user-guide/components/optimized_solutions/libpcl-oneapi.md) [Intel02D_FLANN](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/robotics-ai-suite/1b0b22a2964f0f998c34cc03c5053fe61764015d/docs/user-guide/components/optimized_solutions/libflann-oneapi.md)

策略路线：environment/示教 episode→LeRobot v3→数据统计/训练 checkpoint→选真实 policy 推理入口→export 和可选权重压缩→完整 manifest/model/tokenizer/stats 包→目标设备执行→ActionQueue→本体关节映射/反归一化→控制器。Studio 提供这条资产交接，Runtime 接收 bundle 与部署 YAML，两者用途不同。最需要审查的旧 π0.5 wrapper 调用 forward 并把 actions 作为输入，而当前 LeRobot forward 是训练损失路径；历史 revision 未锁，故本文既不把它写成已验证动作图，也不武断断言历史输出都相同。独立 RTC patch 替换 sample_actions 不能自动修复旧转换教程。[Intel02F_Recording](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/physical-ai-studio/b38bff4a9e6d79481ce1b2bcfdaf3e8ec103724e/application/docs/05-recording-datasets.md) [Intel02F_ExportCode](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/physical-ai-studio/b38bff4a9e6d79481ce1b2bcfdaf3e8ec103724e/library/src/physicalai/export/mixin_policy.py) [Intel02C_ExportCode](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/robotics-ai-suite/1b0b22a2964f0f998c34cc03c5053fe61764015d/src/pipelines/vla-pi0.5-openvino/convert_pytorch_onnx.py) [Intel02C_LeRobot_PI05_Source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/huggingface/lerobot/main/src/lerobot/policies/pi05/modeling_pi05.py) [Intel02C_RTC_Patch](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/robotics-ai-suite/1b0b22a2964f0f998c34cc03c5053fe61764015d/src/pipelines/pi05-rtc-ov/patches/0003-Feat-Enable-ov-inference-in-pi05-policy.patch)

NNCF compress_weights 与 activation 校准量化不同；INT8权重不等于 W8A8。export hook 修改 XML/BIN 后必须重新保存 hash 和动作误差对照。AUTO 默认不纳入 NPU，并可通过 CPU startup/runtime fallback 改变执行设备；精度测试应先显式选择目标设备，再测试回退与并发。HETERO 通过 affinity/query_model 划子图、中间 tensor 交接，分区开销可能使局部加速失效。当前 NPU Compiler-In-Plugin 和 bounded dynamic shape 支持有2026版本、硬件及驱动条件，不能倒套 Suite2025.3。[Intel02B_Weight](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openvinotoolkit/openvino/master/docs/articles_en/openvino-workflow/model-optimization-guide/weight-compression/4-bit-weight-quantization.rst) [Intel02B_AUTO](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openvinotoolkit/openvino/master/docs/articles_en/openvino-workflow/running-inference/inference-devices-and-modes/auto-device-selection.rst) [Intel02B_HETERO](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openvinotoolkit/openvino/master/docs/articles_en/openvino-workflow/running-inference/inference-devices-and-modes/hetero-execution.rst) [Intel02B_NPU](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openvinotoolkit/openvino/master/docs/articles_en/openvino-workflow/running-inference/inference-devices-and-modes/npu-device.rst)

IR 是模型表达，编译 cache 是目标依赖产物，不能替代源模型或跨driver/设备通用文件。CPU共享RAM、USM、remote tensor 也不能自动保证全部buffer共享：NPU远程CPU内存要求指针/size四KB对齐，存活至请求及tensor销毁；GPU context、layout、同步和queue同样是前提。生命周期错误可表现为偶发帧错、结果污染或热负载下失败，光看平均推理毫秒无法发现。[Intel02B_RemoteNPU](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openvinotoolkit/openvino/master/docs/articles_en/openvino-workflow/running-inference/inference-devices-and-modes/npu-device/remote-tensor-api-npu-plugin.rst) [Intel02B_RemoteGPU](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openvinotoolkit/openvino/master/docs/articles_en/openvino-workflow/running-inference/inference-devices-and-modes/gpu-device/remote-tensor-api-gpu-plugin.rst) [Intel02B_Cache](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openvinotoolkit/openvino/master/docs/articles_en/openvino-workflow/running-inference/optimize-inference/optimizing-latency/model-caching-overview.rst)

## 闭环时序、停止与可观测性

ROS传感器 QoS、executor 和软件同步决定观测什么时候被消费。ImageSync 的十毫秒配对门槛不证明曝光硬同步；单侧缺失的队列边界需要审查。FastMapping 的毫米/米深度单位、CameraInfo/TF 决定地图意义；ITS 的持久路网需要失效管理，文档不支持连续重规划。Path 成功、控制器可执行、真实任务完成必须分别测。[Intel02D_ImageSync](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/robotics-ai-suite/1b0b22a2964f0f998c34cc03c5053fe61764015d/src/components/object-detection/src/yolo/src/ImageSync.hpp) [Intel02D_FastMap](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/robotics-ai-suite/1b0b22a2964f0f998c34cc03c5053fe61764015d/docs/user-guide/components/optimized_solutions/run-fastmapping-algorithm.md) [Intel02D_ITS](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/robotics-ai-suite/1b0b22a2964f0f998c34cc03c5053fe61764015d/docs/user-guide/components/navigation/its-path-planner-plugin.md)

Runtime policy调用、chunk消费、插值命令、EtherCAT周期和电机servo是不同速率。RTC通过前缀约束延续队列，不能使后台线程变成硬实时。RobotRuntime stop 在tick之间生效，当前tick仍可能发动作；Studio无control保持最后action，导出还省略grace move。RVC的0.002秒publish与0.1秒timeout、一毫秒RTmotion循环都是配置/样例，不是整机频率或急停认证。应建立独立期限、旧观测拒绝和下游保护。[Intel02C_Runtime_Loop](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openvinotoolkit/physicalai/main/docs/explanation/runtime.md) [Intel02C_Runtime_Modes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openvinotoolkit/physicalai/main/docs/how-to/runtime/use-execution-modes.md) [Intel02F_Sessions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/physical-ai-studio/b38bff4a9e6d79481ce1b2bcfdaf3e8ec103724e/application/docs/explanation/runtime-session-architecture.md) [Intel02E_RVC](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/robotics-ai-suite/1b0b22a2964f0f998c34cc03c5053fe61764015d/docs/user-guide/software_references/stationary_arm/deployment/rvc_deploy.md) [Intel02E_Motion](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/edge-ai-libraries/e5dcdcc705a9306462897de913e39e44701566a1/libraries/edge-control-libraries/plcopen-motion-control/plcopen-motion/README.md)

Profiler 记录消息、资源、GPU和session配置，有助于发现CPU/GPU/控制干扰；处理延迟关联与图表阈值不能代替因果trace或WCET。仿真必须分别记sim clock和wall clock。Pick仿真绕过视觉从Gazebo取得物体位置，SnapFlow性能报告又在H100 Torch上测量，都不能当Intel真实抓取效果。实时调优、cache隔离、IRQ与VFIO是减少干扰的机制，需要准确芯片与拓扑条件；ISO/raw和KVM生命周期并没有提供完整OTA双槽/事务回退证据。[Intel02E_Profiler](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/robotics-ai-suite/1b0b22a2964f0f998c34cc03c5053fe61764015d/docs/user-guide/components/benchmarking/robotics-system-profiler/overview.md) [Intel02E_PickSim](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/robotics-ai-suite/1b0b22a2964f0f998c34cc03c5053fe61764015d/docs/user-guide/software_references/stationary_arm/simulation/picknplace.md) [Intel02F_Benchmark](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/physical-ai-studio/b38bff4a9e6d79481ce1b2bcfdaf3e8ec103724e/library/docs/explanation/benchmark/snapflow-libero.md) [Intel02E_RT](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/robotics-ai-suite/1b0b22a2964f0f998c34cc03c5053fe61764015d/docs/user-guide/components/realtime_determinism/rt_tuning_guide.md) [Intel02A_ImageComposer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/robotics-ai-suite/1b0b22a2964f0f998c34cc03c5053fe61764015d/docs/user-guide/platform_foundation/getting_started/image_composer_tool.md) [Intel02E_KVM](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/robotics-ai-suite/1b0b22a2964f0f998c34cc03c5053fe61764015d/docs/user-guide/components/virtualization/kvm_guide.md)

## 条件选型与交付缺口

分析：已有x86/ROS、PLCopen与ENI资产的团队，可先复用标准数据和动作接口，再选Intel优化节点；重迁CUDA空间计算、其他runtime或本体SDK时，需重做kernel/buffer、预后处理、graph/cache与控制接口验证。没有同条件测量时，本章只比较机制和迁移资产，不给性能排名。公开代码可审查，优势成立在版本锁定、数据契约和观测闭环完整的条件下；多个main分支、旧教程与新组件混装会使这一优势失效。

目前实质缺口包括旧π0.5导出动作语义、NPU/模型逐SKU编译和并发尾延迟、部分内部APT示例的公开可获取性、历史仿真到当前版本的验收、Studio共享store样本一致性、真实本体失败/介入率及OTA恢复事务。代码Apache/MIT不自动覆盖模型Gemma条款、示教/训练数据、固件、第三方库和本体SDK。可复建放行顺序是锁版/SBOM→原模型与导出数值对齐→目标设备冷/热启动→传感器时间/布局→动作单位与坐标→队列/超时/停止注入→仿真留出→本体热稳态任务→更新恢复。当前报告提供这套验证依据和缺口，未替代现场测试与供应商正式交付责任。[Intel02C_Pi05_ModelCard](https://huggingface.co/lerobot/pi05_base/raw/main/README.md) [Intel02D_PCL](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/robotics-ai-suite/1b0b22a2964f0f998c34cc03c5053fe61764015d/docs/user-guide/components/optimized_solutions/libpcl-oneapi.md) [Intel02F_Store](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/physical-ai-studio/b38bff4a9e6d79481ce1b2bcfdaf3e8ec103724e/application/backend/src/runtime_session/store.py) [Intel02F_Update](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-edge-platform/physical-ai-studio/b38bff4a9e6d79481ce1b2bcfdaf3e8ec103724e/application/docs/02-update-existing-installation.md)
